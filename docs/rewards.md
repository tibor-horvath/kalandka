# Rewards

Rewards are only ever added: nothing wilts, nothing is taken away, nothing is random and nothing depends on time played. Stickers come from skill progress, never from the number of tasks; a starting level set by parents (or progress from before the stickers existed) fills the album without a celebration.

**Reward pod.** Next to the level staircase in every game's top bar: a ring of four quarters around a faded seed (a bolt in the workshop), and beside it the seeds or bolts the child has to spend. Each solved task draws in a quarter; the fourth turns the ring gold, the seed colours in and hops into the total, which bumps and counts up by one, and the pod empties (two seconds, played once). A win that also steps up a level plays the level-up first. Solved tasks, not seeds, are the clock the garden grows and the robot's battery runs on, so plants and fruit still move with every task (src/state/pod.ts).

## Számkertész

| Part | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Kiskert | The child's own garden, opened from the eighth plot on the home screen. Seeds are planted in ten beds: a flower (3), a blueberry bush (6) or a pear tree (10). A planting starts as a seedling and grows as the child solves tasks in any game: a flower in 3, a bush in 5, a tree in 8 (shown as dots in rows of five, like a ten-frame). Once all ten are planted a second plot of ten opens (1. kert / 2. kert tabs). |
| Care and Kamra | A grown bush or tree ripens one fruit per solved task, picked one tap at a time and counted aloud. A few tasks after watering it gets thirsty and stops ripening until watered (free; it never wilts). In the Kamra the basket's fruit is counted out into treats for the shelf: Áfonyalekvár (3 berries), Körtebefőtt (3 pears), Gyümölcspite (2 + 2); once all twenty beds are planted, spare seeds bake a Magos kalács (5 seeds). |
| Visitors | A growing garden brings visitors who stay: Süni at 3 grown plants, Katica at 5, a butterfly at 8, a blackbird at 10, Béka at 14 and Mókus at 20. Who comes next shows as a "?" with a ring of one segment per plant still needed since the last visitor, and "még 2" under it; plants planted but still growing show as dotted segments, on the way, and a full ring flashes gold once as the visitor comes. Each is welcomed once with its own screen, and Csepi says which treat they like. |
| Gifts and decorations | Tapping a visitor opens their corner: the treat they ask for and the three things they make in return. Each visitor takes three gifts from the pantry shelf, one treat, then two, then three, counted out aloud: Süni and the butterfly like Áfonyalekvár, Katica Gyümölcspite, Béka Körtebefőtt, the blackbird and Mókus Magos kalács. Every gift leaves a decoration in the visitor's lane for good (Süni: Gomba, Levélkupac, Sünház; Katica: Pöttyös kő, Kis kerítés, Százszorszép-csokor; butterfly: Levendula, Napraforgó, Virágkapu; blackbird: Madáretető, Madáritató, Madárodú; Béka: Kis nádas, Ugróköves ösvény, Kis tó; Mókus: Toboz, Fatönk, Hinta). A visitor whose gift is on the shelf shows that treat in a badge. After all eighteen, a "Kész a kert!" card shows every visitor once; the Kamra keeps working after that. |
| Matricáim | The sticker album, a purple book on each world's island beside the trail, with the number of stickers earned in that world; one tap opens it on that world's page, and Back returns to the island. One sticker per level in every game with levels, earned with the first task solved on that level; Méhkas (16 levels) and Robotút (10) give one per group of neighbouring levels, so no game has more than seven. A sticker once stuck in stays, even if the level steps down. Számmagok has three for the numbers known (5, 10, 20). The Kiskert has a row of its own: Első ajándék, Minden lakónak (every visitor has had a gift) and Kész a kert (all eighteen). Empty places show what comes next; tapping a sticker has the guide say what it is for. Games with seven stickers draw them as a ladder, level 1 at the bottom, without captions. Saves from before the level stickers keep every sticker they had, as the level sticker that took its place. |
| New sticker | Back on the home screen, a newly earned sticker spins in, sparkles, then flies into its place in the game's album page and settles with a squash. Tapping it replays the animation; with reduced motion it simply appears in place. |

## Robotműhely

| Part | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Robotépítő | The child's own robot. Each solved Robotút course and Válogatógép, Mérleg, Mintagép, Tükörgép, Kódzár or Hibavadász round fills a quarter of the reward pod, and every four pay a bolt (csavar); bolts buy parts in six slots (head, antenna, body, arms, legs, paint), 2–9 bolts each. A bought part stays the child's: swapping back is free. Every part on the cards is drawn on the child's own robot, so they see it before buying. |
| Robot friends | Kutyabot at 2 new parts, a drone at 5, Csavarka at 8, Macskabot at 12, Madárbot at 20 and Teknősbot at 30. Who comes next shows as a "?" with a ring of one segment per part still needed since the last friend, and "még 3" under it to count down; a full ring flashes gold once as the friend comes. Each is welcomed once with its own screen and stays on the shelf and in the workshop; tapping one makes it hop. |
| Stickers | Robotműhely has its own album page (the book on each island opens the album on its own world, with a tab for the other). Each robot game has seven level stickers, e.g. Robotút: Első út, Tervező, Hosszú út, Ismétlő, Fordulós, Ismételd és fordulj, Két elem (see `LEVEL_STICKERS` in `src/state/rewards.ts`). Robotépítő has a row of its own, like the Kiskert: Első alkatrész (the first part bought), Második robot (the second robot comes) and Minden barát (all six robot friends). Each world's new stickers pop on its own home screen. |
