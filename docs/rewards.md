# Rewards

Rewards are only ever added: nothing wilts, nothing is taken away and nothing is random. Growing, ripening, thirst and the robot's battery count solved tasks, never minutes, so a week away leaves everything as the child left it. Only two things look at the calendar, both as a gentle daily limit and never as a loss: practice in a finished game pays at most five seeds or bolts a day (below), and each visitor has one snack a day after its five gifts. Stickers come from skill progress, never from the number of tasks; a starting level set by parents (or progress from before the stickers existed) fills the album without a celebration.

**Reward pod.** Next to the level staircase in every game's top bar: a ring around a faded seed (a bolt in the workshop), and beside it the seeds or bolts the child has to spend. In the garden the ring has two halves, so every second solved task pays a seed; in the workshop it has four quarters, a bolt every fourth task. The last part turns the ring gold, the seed colours in and hops into the total, which bumps and counts up by one, and the pod empties (two seconds, played once). A win that also steps up a level plays the level-up first. Solved tasks, not seeds, are the clock the garden grows and the robot's battery runs on, so plants and fruit still move with every task (src/state/pod.ts).

**Practice.** Once every level of a game is done, the game turns into practice with tasks from all its levels. Practice tasks still fill the pod and move the clock, but each game pays at most five seeds or bolts a day (`PRACTICE_PAY_PER_DAY`); after that its pod rests until tomorrow, and the level meter says so. Finished paper pages are not limited.

## Számkertész

| Part | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Kiskert | The child's own garden, opened from the eighth plot on the home screen. One job per view: tabs for the 1. kert, the 2. kert and the Rét sit above the plot next to Csepi, and a dock below it holds Locsolás (only while something is thirsty), Kamra and Magtár. Each plot has fifteen beds in three rows of five. Seeds are planted in them: a flower (3), a blueberry bush (6) or a tree (10). Tapping an empty bed opens a sheet in place of the dock with those three cards; only one sheet is ever open. A planting starts as a seedling and grows as the child solves tasks in any game: a flower in 3, a bush in 5, a tree in 8 (shown as dots in rows of five). Once all fifteen are planted the second plot opens and stays open. Choosing a plant opens the seed count: one hole per seed in rows of five; each tap drops the next seed in, Csepi says its number and the sack counts down. Nothing is spent until the last hole fills. Saves from the ten-bed plots keep every bed in its plot; each plot gets five new empty beds. The beds are never emptied. |
| Care and Kamra | A grown bush or tree ripens one fruit per solved task, picked one tap at a time and counted aloud. A few tasks after watering it gets thirsty and stops ripening until watered (free; it never wilts); thirst shows in the paler leaves and lighter soil with a small drop. The pear tree is every child's; the apple, cherry and walnut trees come with Süni, the blackbird and Mókus (chosen above the seed count, marked "új" until one is planted). The top bar shows one basket pill with all the fruit; tapping it shows each kind. In the Kamra pears and blueberries are counted out into treats for the shelf: Áfonyalekvár (3 berries), Körtebefőtt (3 pears), Gyümölcspite (2 + 2); once both plots have been planted full, spare seeds bake a Magos kalács (5 seeds). Apples, cherries and walnuts go into no recipe: they are given straight to the visitor who loves them. |
| Magtár and Szegély | The Magtár (next to the Kamra) holds five packets of flower seeds: Mályva is every child's, Tulipán (3), Kankalin (4), Búzavirág (5) and Harangvirág (6) are bought once, for good, by counting their seeds in. A flower planted in a bed is of any kind the child has (chosen above the seed count). In front of each open plot runs a border of ten small flowers, beside the Veteményes: tapping a spot selects it (the same blue ring as a bed) and the panel offers the kinds the child has; an empty spot takes one seed, changing a flower's kind is free. When a full row repeats a unit of two to five flowers with two kinds or more (AB, AAB, ABC…), Csepi reads the pattern out once. |
| Veteményes | Five vegetable spots per open plot, beside the border. It opens when the first vegetable visitor arrives: Csiga brings lettuce seed, Nyuszi carrot seed. A spot takes two seeds, is ripe two tasks later with three carrots (or three lettuce heads), and never gets thirsty. Vegetables stay in their spot until their visitor eats them; then the spot is free to plant again. They never go into the basket and never count toward visitors. |
| Visitors | A growing garden brings twelve visitors who stay, counted by beds that have ever grown something (so the count never steps back): Süni at 3, Katica at 5, a butterfly at 8, a blackbird at 10, Béka at 13, Mókus at 16, Csiga at 18, Méhecske at 20, Nyuszi at 22, Vakond at 25, Gyík at 27 and Bagoly at 30. Who comes next shows on the Rét as a "?" with a ring of one segment per plant still needed, and "még 2" under it; plants still growing show as dotted segments, and a full ring flashes gold once as the visitor comes. Each is welcomed once with its own screen, which also shows the seeds it brought ("Nyuszi répamagot hozott!"). Once Méhecske has come a few bees sit still on the flowers (one on every third), and once the butterfly has come it sits on one flower; tapping that flower makes the guest hop once. |
| Gifts and decorations | The Rét gives every visitor a corner: the animal, its decorations, five gift dots, or a "kér" badge with what it wants once a gift is ready. Tapping a corner opens its gift card. Each visitor takes five gifts. A treat from the pantry shelf is one, then two, up to five: Süni, the butterfly, Méhecske and Nyuszi like Áfonyalekvár, Katica, Csiga and Gyík Gyümölcspite, Béka and Vakond Körtebefőtt, the blackbird, Mókus and Bagoly Magos kalács. Visitors who love something from the garden are offered it first, three for each treat (3, 6 … 15): Nyuszi carrots and Csiga lettuce, eaten in the Veteményes bite by bite while Csepi counts; Süni apples, the blackbird cherries and Mókus walnuts, straight from the basket; Méhecske pollen, gathered by the bee from flower to flower in the beds and the border by itself while Csepi counts (every flower stays, and has pollen again three tasks later). The three birds also take a handful of seeds instead, counted out like a planting. Beds are never emptied, so a garden planted full without a bush (or without a pear tree) could never make the treats that need berries (or pears) again; then every visitor who likes such a treat takes a handful of seeds too, the same way as the birds, so every gift can still be given (`treatOutOfReach`, `takesSeeds`). Every gift leaves a decoration in the visitor's corner for good, sixty in all. After all sixty, a "Kész a kert!" card shows every visitor once; the Kamra keeps working after that. |
| Snacks and piles | After its five gifts, every visitor still has a snack once a day, the smallest gift in any form it takes: three of its garden food (eaten in the garden while Csepi counts, like a gift), one treat from the shelf, or three seeds counted out for those who take seeds. Each snack adds one small thing to the visitor's pile, drawn in front of its feet on the Rét and as ten places in rows of five on its gift card: Süni mushrooms, Katica poppies, the butterfly lavender, the blackbird cherry branches, Béka water lilies, Mókus pinecones, Csiga mossy stones, Méhecske honey pots, Nyuszi clover, Vakond molehills, Gyík ferns and Bagoly feathers (`PILE_OF`, each one of its own decorations). The newest one pops in once and Csepi counts it ("Már három!"). Snacks go on once the pile holds ten; it simply stays full. A snack is only ever offered on a tap, never as a reminder or a "kér" badge. |
| Matricáim | The sticker album, a purple book on each world's island beside the trail, with the number of stickers earned in that world; one tap opens it on that world's page, and Back returns to the island. One sticker per level in every game with levels, earned with the first task solved on that level, in the game or on a finished sheet, in any order: a higher level earns its own sticker, never the ones below it. Méhkas (16 levels) and Robotút (10) once had one per group of neighbouring levels; saves from then swap each group sticker for the stickers of the levels done in it, with no new pops. A sticker once stuck in stays, even if the level steps down. Számmagok has three for the numbers known (5, 10, 20). The Kiskert has a row of its own: Első ajándék, Minden lakónak (all twelve visitors have had a gift) and Kész a kert (all sixty). Feladatlapok, the printed sheets marked done in that world, has nine, at the Fibonacci numbers 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34 and 55 pages: each is drawn as a page with its number on it (a stack from five on) and the world's sign in the corner, the last with a gold rim; it and Robotút's ten are drawn smaller, and Méhkas's sixteen and Robotút's ten stand in two columns without the line. The old fifteen-page sticker carries over as the thirteen-page one. Empty places show what comes next; tapping a sticker has the guide say what it is for. Games with seven stickers draw them as a ladder, level 1 at the bottom, without captions. Saves from before the level stickers keep every sticker they had, as the level sticker that took its place. |
| New sticker | Back on the home screen, a newly earned sticker spins in, sparkles, then flies into its place in the game's album page and settles with a squash. Tapping it replays the animation; with reduced motion it simply appears in place. |

## Robotműhely

| Part | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Robotépítő | The child's own robot. Each solved Robotút course and Válogatógép, Mérleg, Mintagép, Tükörgép, Kódzár or Hibavadász round fills a quarter of the reward pod, and every four pay a bolt (csavar); bolts buy parts in six slots (head, antenna, body, arms, legs, paint), 2–9 bolts each. A bought part stays the child's: swapping back is free. Every part on the cards is drawn on the child's own robot, so they see it before buying. A tab with a part the bolts buy carries a small bolt dot, and the workshop opens on the wished part's tab, else the first such tab, else Fej (`startSlot`). Tapping a part the bolts reach opens the bolt count, like a planting: one screw hole per bolt in rows of five, each tap on the box or the tray drops the next bolt in, Csavar says its number and the box counts down; nothing is spent until the last hole fills, and Back keeps every bolt. The island's workshop spot says "Építhetsz!" whenever a part or a friend's gift is within the bolts. |
| Ezt szeretném | Tapping a part the bolts do not reach yet makes it the one the child saves up for: Csavar says so, and the card wears a heart. One wish at a time (another tap replaces it); buying it clears it. In every workshop game a heart beside the bolt total shows "még 6", or "megvan" in gold once the balance reaches it, and Csavar says so once, after the bolt that made it enough (`wishOf`). |
| Robot friends | Kutyabot at 2 new parts, a drone at 5, Csavarka at 8, Macskabot at 12, Madárbot at 20 and Teknősbot at 30. Who comes next shows as a "?" with a ring of one segment per part still needed since the last friend, and "még 3" under it to count down; a full ring flashes gold once as the friend comes. Each is welcomed once with its own screen and stays on the shelf and in the workshop; tapping one makes it hop. |
| Barátok műhelye | Once a robot on the workbench has every part, the welcomed friends ask for gifts: each wears a "kér" tag, and a tap opens its card with five gift places. Each friend asks for five different gifts in turn (a ball, a bowl, a bell…), the first for one bolt, then two, up to five, counted out like a part. Every gift stays beside its friend in the workshop for good, thirty in all (`FRIEND_GIFTS`). |
| Stickers | Robotműhely has its own album page (the book on each island opens the album on its own world, with a tab for the other). Each robot game has a sticker per level: seven, and Robotút ten (Első út, Dobozok között, Tervező, Kerülő terv, Hosszú út, Ismétlő, Fordulós, Fordulj, kerüld ki, Ismételd és fordulj, Két elem) (see `LEVEL_STICKERS` in `src/state/rewards.ts`). Robotépítő has a row of its own, like the Kiskert: Első alkatrész (the first part bought), Második robot (the second robot comes), Minden barát (all six robot friends), Minden barátnak (every friend has had a gift) and Teli polc (all thirty gifts). Each world's new stickers pop on its own home screen. |
