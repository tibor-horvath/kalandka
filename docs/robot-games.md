# Robotműhely games

The robot workshop for the bigger ones, guided by Csavar the robot. How each game steps up and down is in [Levels](levels.md); the bolts, the robot and its friends are in [Rewards](rewards.md#robotműhely).

| Game | Skill | How it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Robotút | Algorithmic thinking, planning, debugging | Lead the little robot across the workshop floor to its battery. Ten levels, five courses each: stepping with arrows (each tap moves at once), with crates in the way; planning a whole program of arrow cards and pressing Indulás; planning round crates; long courses; repeat cards (×2, ×3) with arrows; turning the way the robot faces (Előre, Fordulj balra/jobbra); turning round crates; repeats with turns; two batteries in one program. On the advanced levels a battery under the floor limits the program to the shortest way plus one or two steps: teal cells are charge, yellow what the plan will use; a plan too long shows orange ghost cells past the battery. When the charge runs out the robot sputters, leans over and dims, and orange dots show the steps it was short of. Courses are generated from the level and round and always checked to be possible. A bump plays once (the robot squashes against the crate or wall, sparks, a crate wobbles or the wall edge flashes) and stops the robot; Csavar names the card ("Az első kártyánál…"), and the robot rolls back to the start; nothing fails. "Mutasd az utat!" dots the way on the floor. A finished course shows "Feltöltve!" and fills a quarter of the bolt pod. |
| Válogatógép | Sorting and classifying, finding a rule | Parts come down the belt one at a time and the child taps the box each belongs in (the parts still to come wait in closed crates, so nothing on the belt looks "small" by comparison). Tapping the part has Csavar say what it is ("nagy piros kör"). Seven levels: two colours with nothing else changing (for 5-year-olds), three colours, shapes, big and small; Kakukktojás (three rows of four parts, tap the one that is different; exactly one feature singles it out); Két szabály (one box for one colour and shape together, e.g. red circles, the other for the rest, with near misses); Mi a szabály? (the boxes have no labels, Csavar puts in two examples that differ only in the rule, the child carries on). A part in the wrong box gets "Hoppá" and Csavar names what to look at; after a second try the right box lights up (the odd part after a third). A finished round shows "Kész!" and fills a quarter of the bolt pod. Colours differ in lightness too (light yellow, mid red, dark blue). |
| Mérleg | More, fewer, the same; balancing; missing addends | A balance scale whose beam tips towards the heavier pan (more difference, more tilt). Seven levels: Melyik a nehezebb? (the beam is held level by two props; the child says which side will go down, 3–5 weights against at least two fewer, for 5-year-olds), then "or the same" up to 8; Tedd egyensúlyba! and Egyensúly tízig (put weights on the right pan, or take them off with Levesz, until the beam levels out); Kettes súlyok (a wide weight counts two); Hiányzó súly (pick the weight missing from the right pan); Titkos doboz (pick what the closed crate on the left weighs). A wrong compare answer has Csavar count both sides with the child, the counts showing over the pans; a wrong weight goes on the pan, the scale tips so the child sees too much or too little, then it comes off. After three tries the right answer lights up. Three tasks a round (two when balancing); a finished round fills a quarter of the bolt pod. |
| Mintagép | Harder patterns, finding the rule | Parts ride the belt in a row that follows a rule; the child picks what fills the gap from three cards. Seven levels (four tasks a round, three on the last two levels): Két szín (red-blue-red-blue, one shape, for 5-year-olds); Hosszabb ismétlés (ABC, AAB, ABB, AABB); Szín és forma (colour and shape repeat on cycles of two and three, each wrong card off by one feature); Növekvő sor (towers growing by one, then by two); Forgó sor (an arrow turning a quarter each step, clockwise or back, later changing colour as well); Hibavadász (one part out of place after two whole repeats: tap it, it lifts off the belt, then pick what belongs there); Mintafordító (the pattern in colours above, the same pattern in arrows below, one arrow per colour). The row always shows at least two whole repeats. A right card lands in the gap with a green tick on the gap and the card, and Csavar reads the finished row aloud. A wrong card sits in the gap a moment with an orange cross, then goes back and stays dimmed; Csavar reads the row up to the gap and brackets mark each repeat. After two misses the right card lights up (after three, on Hibavadász, the part out of place). Tapping a part has Csavar name it. A finished round shows "Kész!" and fills a quarter of the bolt pod. |
| Tükörgép | Mirroring and symmetry | A lamp board with a mirror across it: what shines on one side has to shine on the other, just as far from the mirror. Seven levels (three tasks a round, two on the picture levels): Egy lámpa (one lamp, tap where its reflection goes, for 5-year-olds); Több lámpa (two to four lamps); Fél kép (half a picture to finish: a heart, a robot, a rocket, a house, a butterfly, a flower; whole, Csavar names it); Színes kép (the same pictures in their colours: pick a paint pot, then tap); Fel-le tükör (the mirror lies flat, top to bottom); Tükör vagy másolat? (pick the true reflection of a shape from a plain copy and a quarter turn); Hibás tükör (the machine lit the other half with one lamp missing or one too many: tap the place). A right lamp lights with a pop; a whole board makes the mirror flash once. A wrong lamp blinks orange and goes out, and Csavar counts the steps from the mirror ("két lépésre van a tükörtől"); after two misses the lamp and its place across the mirror glow, with dots between them. "Mutasd!" shows the next pair the same way. A wrong card sits in the frame a moment and stays dimmed. A finished round shows "Kész!" and fills a quarter of the bolt pod. |
| Kódzár | Signs and codes, reading a code key, encoding and decoding | A crate shut with a code lock: the lock shows the code in signs, and each keypad press fills the next slot, left to right. Seven levels (three tasks a round, two on the last): Színzár (copy two colour lamps, for 5-year-olds); Hosszabb zár (three or four lamps, repeats allowed); Kódtábla (a code key beside the crate says what each lamp means, the keypad shows pictures: fogaskerék, elem, villáskulcs, mágnes); Új kódtábla (three or four signs, the key mixed up for every task, a fourth picture on the keypad that no lamp means); Pöttykód (dice faces to numerals, with a faded dots → numeral key); Üzenet (a picture message for Kutyabot, turned into lamps by reading the key backwards; Kutyabot fetches the first picture); Hiányzó jel (one key row covered: a note stuck beside the crate shows two lamps and their meanings, the child picks the covered meaning from three cards, then opens the lock). Tapping a sign or a key row has Csavar name it. A wrong button sits in the slot a moment with an orange cross and pops back out; Csavar names what to look at ("Nézd meg a második lámpát!", "Keresd meg a kék lámpát a kódtáblán!", or counts the dots aloud) and that key row glows once. After a second miss on a slot the right button lights up; filled slots are never cleared. "Mutasd!" lights the sign, its key row and the right button. The last slot opens the crate once (the shackle lifts, the lid tips, a bolt inside); a finished round shows "Kész!" and fills a quarter of the bolt pod. On an upright phone the key goes above the crate when they do not fit side by side. |
| Hibavadász | Finding and fixing bugs in a program | The robot's program is already laid out, but it does not reach the battery. The child presses Indulás and watches: the card running lights up and the robot leaves an orange trail on the floor, then rolls back to the start. They tap the card to blame, it lifts, and they pick what belongs there; the robot tries again. Seven levels (three tasks a round, two on the last): Rossz nyíl (one wrong arrow on a small floor, with the right way dotted, for 5-year-olds); Hosszabb program (more cards, a crate or two, still dotted); Dobozok között (no dots); Felesleges kártya (one card too many: tap it and it comes out); Rossz szám (a repeat with the wrong number: pick ×1, ×2 or ×3); Fordulós hiba (the robot goes the way it faces); Két hiba (two wrong cards, one at a time, the robot getting further after the first). Every task is built so exactly one change puts it right. A card that is fine wobbles and stays dimmed, and Csavar says which way to look ("Ez a kártya jó, idáig jól ment a robot."); a wrong pick is run, so the child sees it does not get there, and stays dimmed. After two misses the wrong card or the right choice lights up. "Mutasd!" does the same and dots the way on the floor. A charged battery and a green card show each task done; a finished round shows "Kész!" and fills a quarter of the bolt pod. |
