# Számkertész games

The counting garden, guided by Csepi the water drop. How each game steps up and down is in [Levels](levels.md); what a solved task pays is in [Rewards](rewards.md).

| Game | Skill | How it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locsolás | Counting 1–20, counting on, crossing ten, counting in twos | Tap the cloud to water the plant with exactly N drops. Csepi counts aloud; too much water gets a gentle "Hoppá". Six levels: 2–5 drops, 6–10 drops (one ten-frame), then 11–20 (two ten-frames) where a watering can pours 5 at once. Then Továbbszámolás: some drops are already in the frame (paler, and they can't be taken away) and the child counts on 2–6 more without crossing ten; Tízes átlépés: 6–9 drops are there and the plant needs 11–17, so the way over is to fill the first frame; Kettesével: even targets 6–20, the cloud gives two drops a tap (no can) and Csepi says only the totals, "kettő, négy, hat". |
| Virágsor | Patterns (AB, ABC, AAB, ABB, AABB, growing by one or two) | Pick the flower that continues the row. Seven levels: AB, ABC, then AAB and ABB, the missing flower moved into the row ("Melyik virág hiányzik?"), then AABB, and last two levels of growing bunches: each bunch has one more flower than the one before (from 1, 2 or 3), then two more (1, 3, 5, 7 or 2, 4, 6, 8), drawn in rows of two up to ten flowers with the count under each; the child picks the next bunch by its size. A wrong pick there reads the counts back, then names the last bunch and the step. The printed sheets stop at the missing-flower level. A wrong pick reads back a whole repeat the gap doesn't touch, then the whole row with the gap. |
| Számmagok | Meeting the numbers 1–20 | Open from the start. Numbers come in order; the child taps each seed so it sprouts while Csepi counts, then the numeral, its dots and its word appear. No timer. 6–10 show as a five plus the rest, 11–20 as a full ten-frame plus the rest. |
| Villámszem | Recognising amounts at a glance | Opens after numbers 1–5 in Számmagok. Seeds show for 2.5–3 s, then the child picks how many; "Mutasd újra!" shows them again any time. A wrong pick uncovers the seeds and counts them together. Six levels: 1–3 and 1–5 as dice faces, 1–5 scattered, 6–10 as a five and the rest, then two ten-frame levels: 6–10 in one big ten-frame and 11–20 as a full ten-frame and a second one, both with the empty spaces drawn so a missing seed shows as a gap. There a wrong pick has Csepi read the frame instead of counting one by one ("Egy teli tízes meg még hét: ez tizenhét"). The printed sheets keep the first four levels. |
| Méhkas | Addition and subtraction, 5 to 100 | Opens after numbers 1–10 in Számmagok. Two groups of flowers, "a + b = ?", pick the total; on the take levels the child taps b of the a flowers, the bee carries them to the hive, and they pick how many are left ("a − b = ?"). Tap a flower and the bee counts it; a wrong pick sends the bee round everything, counting aloud. Sixteen levels, adding and taking away in turn (up to 5, up to 10 gathering or counting on from a basket, up to 20, crossing ten, round tens, up to 100, up to 100 with carrying); from 20 on a bouquet stands for ten. The level follows the child (see [Levels](levels.md)): five first-try answers in a row step up, three sums in a row that needed help quietly step down. |
| Mézút | Writing the digits 0–9 | Open from the start. The child leads the bee along a dotted honey trail in writing order (1–9, then 0); 4, 5 and 7 take two strokes. The trail is forgiving: the finger may wander within a soft lane, and the honey always follows the trail, never the wobble. Off the lane nothing fails: the bee waits, the next stretch glows, a thread leads back and Csepi says "Gyere vissza a mézes útra!". Honey drops pop with a rising bubble sound (and a light vibration where supported); a see-through bee shows the way at the start, on "Mutasd az utat!" and after a quiet spell. Four levels: 1–5 and 6–0 on the full trail, then both halves as Halvány út: the digits in mixed order on a faint trail (thin dots and a few arrows, no lane or honey drops to follow) and no see-through bee at the start, so the child writes from memory; the finger is followed just as forgivingly. |
| Cserépbarátok | Number friends of 5–10 and 20 | Opens after numbers 1–10 in Számmagok. A window box of 5 or 10 pots (rows of five, like a ten-frame); some already flower, and the child picks how many more fill the box. Tapping an empty pot plants a sprout and Csepi counts it; a wrong pick has Csepi count the empty pots together. A right answer makes them bloom in a second colour and shows "3 + 7 = 10". Six levels: friends of five, friends of ten in order, friends of ten scattered, then friends of six to nine (boxes of 6, 7, 8 and 9 in turn), friends of twenty (the first ten always full, so it is the friends of ten again in the second ten) and last friends of ten under a cover: the box is hidden and only "7 / 10 cserép" shows, so the child answers from memory; a wrong pick takes the cover off and Csepi counts the empty pots. The printed sheets keep the first three levels. |

Every solved task fills a quarter of the reward pod in the game's top bar, and every four pay a seed (see [Rewards](rewards.md)); each watered plant is added to the garden on the home screen.
