# For parents

## Parents' area

The parents' area opens through a gate (a two-digit multiplication such as 6 × 9 on a keypad, which 5–7 year olds cannot do yet). It is a separate full-screen space with a menu: **Áttekintés** (today's time ring, the last 7 days, plain-language notes on what is going well or hard, progress per game; every game keeps its last few solved tasks and whether each needed help, so each game gets a note: going well, could use help, or too little play this week, with an idea to try at home), **Gyerkőcök** (up to six children on one device, each with their own figure, age, garden, levels and play time; whether Csepi asks "Ki játszik?" at start; removing a child), **Képernyőidő** (daily limit, +10 minutes, rest now, warning and finish-the-task switches, evening rest), **Szintek** (a starting level per game with −/+ steppers, all games open), **Nyomtatás** (below) and **Beállítások** (Csepi's voice and speaking speed, sound effects, privacy, moving every child to a new device by QR codes (Átköltözés új eszközre), a two-step progress reset).

## Printable sheets

**Nyomtatás** (printable sheets): every game as an A4 worksheet for play without a device, one game per printout. It is in the parents' area, and also open without the gate from a Nyomtatás button on Ki játszik? and on both home screens (a full-screen Feladatlapok view with a child switch; like the parents' area it pauses the play clock). The tasks come from the games' own generators at the child's current level (a parent can pick another), and tapping becomes circling, colouring, drawing a line or crossing out: Számmagok shows the number (in the shape Mézút teaches) and the child colours that many seeds, Locsolás colours N drops in a ten-frame, Mézút traces each digit on a dotted trail with start dots and arrows and then writes it alone, Robotút draws the route and the program cards, Válogatógép joins each part to its box, Tükörgép colours the reflected lamps, Kódzár draws or colours the meaning into the box under each sign (on Hiányzó jel the covered key row first), Hibavadász crosses out the wrong card and draws the right one under it, and so on. Parents choose how many tasks go on a page (Kevesebb, nagyobb / Normál / Sok, kisebb) and up to five pages, each with new tasks. Every task has a star to colour when it is done, where colour is the task (Válogatógép, Mintagép, Tükörgép's coloured pictures, Kódzár's lamps) red is also striped and blue dotted, with a key on the sheet, so black-and-white printers work too; and every page carries the Kalandka logo, the world's name and the address the app runs at. The answers show only on screen (Feladatlap / Megoldások), never on paper; the last choice per child and game is kept on the device so a finished sheet can be checked later (src/print).

**Kész lap** (marking a page done): a grown-up marks a finished sheet in Nyomtatás, or scans its QR code with the tablet's camera (Lap beolvasása) to see the page's own answers and tick them off. A page counts once, for finishing, never for how many answers were right, and changes no level (once every page of a printout is marked done, the game's sheet opens on new tasks next time, so the next printout counts again): its tasks fill the child's reward pod like solved tasks in the games, so a 9-task page with a half-full pod pays two seeds. After a scan the parents' page plays it back once: the button turns green with a tick, the page's tasks drop one by one into the child's pod, every full pod sends a seed (or bolt) into their total, then a plain summary and any new sticker: Feladatlapok (at 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34 and 55 pages in each world), and the game's own level sticker for the level the page was printed on, as if that level were solved in the game; with reduced motion it shows the end straight away (src/screens/parents/PageReceipt.tsx).

**Kellékek** (cut-out pages, at the bottom of Nyomtatás): three A4 pages that are the same for every child, so they have no level, no QR code and nothing to mark, only a preview and a print button. *Csepi és Csavar* are fold-in-half tent cards that stand beside a worksheet while the grown-up reads the task in their voice; *Jelvények* has 12 Kész!, 6 Kitartó! (stuck but finished) and 6 Bátor! (tried a new game or a harder level) badges to stick on a finished sheet; *Kis robot és parancskártyák* has the Robotút robot in three sizes (pick the one that fits one square of the sheet's grid, which changes with level and density) and cut-out direction and turn cards for laying out a program and stepping it on paper. The Robotút sheet links to the robot page, and the parents' "Kész lap megjelölése" card links to the badges (src/print/extras.tsx).

## Screen time

Only active play counts (app visible, parents' area closed) and the count starts again each day. Two minutes before the end a badge appears and Csepi says so; at the limit a child in a game may finish the task (up to 2 minutes), then a rest screen appears that only the parents' gate dismisses. "Pihenő most" ends play for today; evening rest blocks play from 18–20:00 until 7:00. Resetting progress does not reset today's play time.
